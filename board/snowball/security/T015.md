## Outcome

Remove `sb export` and passphrase re-encryption. Preserve analytical SQL, legitimate PNG output, and the existing device-key encrypted format. Caller SQL must be evaluated separately from the owned writer. Persistent dataset output must stay inside the managed data directory, and only complete saves may become visible through the registry.

## Scope

- CLI and docs: `sb.py`, `sbcore/cmd_export.py` (removal), `SKILL.md`, `references/query-play.md`.
- Reader, evaluator, chart and storage boundaries: `sbcore/cmd_play.py`, `sbcore/cmd_chart.py`, `sbcore/sqlguard.py`, `sbcore/store.py`.
- Focused regressions: `tests/test_export_removed.py`, `tests/test_dataset_paths.py`, `tests/test_docs_coverage.py`, `tests/test_t132.py`. Document any additional native build or test paths before changing them.
- Native boundary increment: `native/t015/CMakeLists.txt`, `native/t015/pinned_files.hpp`, `native/t015/pinned_files.cpp`, and `native/t015/pinned_files_test.cpp` for Windows handle acquisition and synthetic verification. This increment does not wire the product reader or modify packaging.
- Out of scope: MCP, unrelated security tickets, auth changes, live data, migrations or deletion of existing datasets, and enabling any new export channel.

## Outcome
memb links chunks to other chunks (cosine >= 0.72) and to existing notes (>= 0.70) automatically and without a model, builds complete evidence packets from the links, and curation is triggered after the daily sweep or when 3 uncurated chunks exist; a packet already judged is not sent again. Source: Phase 3 section 1 Layer 2, shared plan section 2, Phase 4 pipeline step 2. Depends on the Layer 1 summary/embedding ticket.


## Scope
memb-curator packet building, link storage in redb, trigger logic in the daemon and `memb curate`, CURATION_HASH_CACHE. DEFER keeps chunks staged for the 30-day limit. Out of scope: the model call and applying edits.

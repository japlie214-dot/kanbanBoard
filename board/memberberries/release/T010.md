## Outcome
CORRECTED 2026-11-25. This ticket delivers only the BUILDING BLOCKS of Layer 2 curation, not the pipeline in Phase 3/4: (a) a bounded runner for the user's command-line model (`curator.cli_command`, MEMB_INTERNAL=1, no shell, time/output limits); (b) `memb curate` which sends staged chunk TEXT (no summaries, no vectors, no clustering) to that command and records its replies as pending proposals; (c) update and retract proposals with host-stamped source_chunks (retract cannot cascade); (d) a durable cross-process proposal list with single-use claim and typed-confirmation apply (`memb proposals`); (e) a 30-day staging limit (curator.staging_ttl_days) on staged chunks and pending proposals.

# T035 — `execute_buys` buys UPRO as well as SCHG, with the right instrument, identity and book, and recovery rebuilds each symbol correctly

## Outcome
`execute_buys` accepts open intents for any symbol in `instrument_ids` with a positive amount. Instrument id, intent/order symbol and annotation symbol all come from `intent.symbol`. The book comes from `intent.book` ('catapult' or 'reentry'; SCHG has no book from the ladder, so it defaults to 'catapult'). `recover_unfinished` rebuilds the `LadderIntent` from the intent row's own symbol and book.

## Why
- Consultant 2026-10-24 (ticket C) and precheck T033: SCHG is hardcoded throughout, so widening only the guard would send a UPRO intent as SCHG.
- The ladder emits UPRO opens: catapult ladder buys, and reentry tranches with `book="reentry"` (`P/strategy/ladder.py:745-748`).

## Design (PM decisions)
1. **Guard:** `side == "open"`, `symbol in instrument_ids`, `amount is not None and amount > 0`; else ValueError(`"executes opens of known symbols only: ..."`).
2. **`_buy_one`:** `instrument_id = instrument_ids[intent.symbol]`. Intent row `symbol=intent.symbol`, `book=intent.book or "catapult"`. Order row `symbol=intent.symbol`.
3. **`finish_filled_buy` annotation:**
   - `symbol=intent.symbol`, `book=intent.book or "catapult"`;
   - `bucket_id`: for book 'reentry', parse with `re.fullmatch(r"deferred reentry (\S+) \d+/3", intent.reason)`; on no match leave None and add the followup `"reentry fill without bucket id: <reason>"`;
   - SPY fields unchanged.
   - TP clear applies to every symbol (Boss decision B applies to all buys).
4. **Recovery :601:** `LadderIntent(intent.symbol, "open", amount=intent.amount_usd, reason=intent.reason or "", book=intent.book)`.
5. **Docstrings:** "SCHG and UPRO open buys". There are no other behaviour changes, and the order of steps is unchanged.

# Fund-ladder SCHG sell must not starve the UPRO buy it funds

## Outcome

Under a small order ceiling, a funding SCHG sell is only sent if the UPRO buy it funds can also be sent; a shortfall doesn't block buys for ever.

## Scope

engine/session.py, close_executor.py, strategy/ladder.py, tests.

## Acceptance

Audit S6/S8: closes go first (session.py:714-716,788-818) and with max_orders_per_session=1 the funding sell uses the only send, the UPRO buy is deferred and cash idles; shortfall sets blocks_buys and vetoes save every session (session.py:815-816). Design reviewed by PM.

## Outcome
Telegram commands (/dashboard, /status, /help...) get a reply within minutes on Z10. Found 2026-11-24: the bot reads Telegram only at the start of an armed or dry session (engine/session.py, notify/poll.process_pending). config.toml says commands are polled hourly by a TelegramPollReceiver, but no such receiver exists in the Android or Python code, so replies can take up to a day. /start is not a supported command; the help text lists the supported ones.


## Scope
android-probe (alarm or worker that calls a Python poll entry), notify/poll.py entry point, config.toml comment, tests. Not the strategy, not order paths.

- [x] A4. [ACCEPTANCE] [lane:qa] [after:A3] Telegram queue drained and a reply received. Timed round trip: /dashboard sent 16:37 local, reply received 16:51 local (about 14 minutes). The poll at 16:36:56 failed with DNS "No address associated with hostname" just after mobile data reconnected at 16:36:54; the next scheduled poll (about 16:51) answered. Earlier polls at 15:39, 15:59 and 16:19 failed the same way. Boss decision 2026-11-24: the 14-minute reply and the DNS-failed polls with no retry are tolerated for this ticket; the Prove threshold is accepted as "reply within the next scheduled poll cycle".
